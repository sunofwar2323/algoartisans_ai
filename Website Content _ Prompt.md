# **ALGOARTISANS — WEBSITE DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT PROMPT**

Redesign and build a premium, modern website for **AlgoArtisans**, an AI-native technology company founded by two technology professionals.

## **Brand**

**Company:** AlgoArtisans  
**Primary tagline:** Build. Automate. Scale.  
**Positioning:** Human-led. AI-powered.  
**Industry:** AI, Software Engineering, SaaS, Automation, Research

AlgoArtisans builds digital systems, applications, AI agents, intelligent automation and research systems for businesses, organizations and ambitious founders.

The company operates with two human founders supported by a specialized AI workforce.

---

# **CORE BRAND IDEA**

AlgoArtisans is not a traditional software agency.

It is an **AI-native technology company** where human expertise and AI-powered teams work together to design, build and deploy technology.

The website should communicate:

* Engineering excellence  
* Artificial intelligence  
* Software craftsmanship  
* Speed  
* Precision  
* Innovation  
* Trust  
* Premium technology  
* Human \+ AI collaboration

The website should NOT feel like a generic AI startup.

Avoid:

* Generic robot illustrations  
* Excessive glowing AI graphics  
* Stock photos of programmers  
* Overused blue/purple AI gradients  
* Excessive animations  
* Cheap SaaS-template aesthetics  
* Too much text above the fold

Instead, create a sophisticated visual language inspired by premium technology companies, engineering laboratories and modern software studios.

---

# **VISUAL DIRECTION**

Create a dark, sophisticated interface with strong typography and generous spacing.

Use:

* Deep black / near-black background  
* White and soft-gray typography  
* One distinctive brand accent color  
* Subtle gradients  
* Fine grid patterns  
* Technical diagrams  
* Abstract algorithmic visuals  
* Minimal motion  
* Smooth transitions  
* Glass or translucent elements only where appropriate

Typography should be modern, technical and highly readable.

The website should feel:  
**Intelligent. Precise. Experimental. Premium.**

# **UX REQUIREMENTS**

The website must be:

* Fully responsive  
* Mobile-first  
* Fast-loading  
* SEO optimized  
* Accessible  
* Semantic HTML  
* High performance  
* Clean component architecture  
* Easy to maintain  
* CMS-ready for future case studies and research articles

Animations should be subtle and intentional.

Use smooth scroll transitions, hover interactions, card reveals and lightweight motion.

Avoid excessive animation that negatively impacts performance.

---

# **DESIGN PRINCIPLE**

Every section should answer one question:

**Why should a serious business trust AlgoArtisans to build its technology?**

The final website should feel like a company that could confidently work with startups, enterprises, government organizations and ambitious founders.

It should feel established even though the company is intentionally lean.

The visual experience should communicate:

**Precision × Intelligence × Engineering × Speed**

Final brand impression:

**AlgoArtisans is not an agency that uses AI.**  
**AlgoArtisans is an AI-native company built around intelligent systems.**
